# Audio credits

Every file here is CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication); credit is not required but given
gladly. Each licence page was checked on 2026-10-18 and lists CC0 as the only licence.
Files were cut to loops (with a short crossfade at the seam), mixed to mono, level-matched
and encoded to MP3 with ffmpeg. They load only after the visitor turns sound on.
